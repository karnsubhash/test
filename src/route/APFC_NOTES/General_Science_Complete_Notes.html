General Science — Comprehensive Notes

UPSC EPFO (APFC / EO-AO) · for the 2026 exam

 

General Science ≈ 50 marks across the last two papers. It blends STATIC fundamentals (physics, chemistry, biology, environment — Sections 1–4) with SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY current affairs (Section 5, which needs updating for 2026). Level is applied/conceptual (Class 10-ish) plus tech awareness (defence, space, AI).

1 · Physics

Mechanics

●        Equations of motion: v = u + at ; s = ut + ½at² ; v² = u² + 2as. (e.g. train from rest to 30 m/s in 300 s → s = ½·30·300 = 4500 m)

●        Momentum & collisions: momentum is ALWAYS conserved in a collision; in an INELASTIC collision kinetic energy is NOT conserved (in perfectly elastic, both are conserved)

●        Newton's laws / work-energy: F = ma; work = force × displacement; power = work/time; KE = ½mv²; PE = mgh

Optics

Concept

Key point

Lens power

P = 1/f (in metres), unit dioptre (D); combined power = P₁ + P₂. Net negative → diverging (concave) lens; net positive → converging (convex)

Concave mirror — object at infinity

real, inverted, highly diminished image formed at the focus (F)

Convex lens/mirror uses

convex mirror → rear-view (wider field); concave mirror → headlights, shaving, telescopes

Refraction

bending of light between media; total internal reflection → optical fibres, mirage

2 · Chemistry

Basic & organic chemistry

Item

Key point

Oxidation / reduction

oxidation = loss of electrons / increase in oxidation state; reduction = gain / decrease. In 2H₂+O₂→2H₂O, H goes 0→+1 (oxidised), O goes 0→−2 (reduced)

Fatty acids

have a carboxyl group (–COOH) at one end and a methyl group (–CH₃) at the other; saturated = no C=C double bonds

Phenol

commonly called Carbolic acid

Amines

chief commercial use — synthesis of medicines (drugs) and synthetic fibres (e.g. nylon)

Soaps

sodium/potassium salts of long-chain fatty acids (sodium stearate, palmate, oleate). Sodium benzoate is a PRESERVATIVE, not a soap

Acids/bases

pH < 7 acidic, > 7 basic; strong vs weak; neutralisation gives salt + water

Environmental chemistry

Item

Key point

Photochemical smog

primary reactants = Nitric Oxide (NOx) and unburnt hydrocarbons, reacting under sunlight (oxidising smog, forms ozone/PAN)

Ocean acidification

mainly caused by dissolved CO₂ forming carbonic acid → lowers ocean pH

Water hardness

determined by the total concentration of Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ ions in water

Industrial effluents & pH

lead-acid battery effluents contain sulphuric acid → sharply lower (acidify) river water pH

Greenhouse gases

CO₂, methane (CH₄), N₂O, water vapour, CFCs; global warming potential varies

3 · Biology

Human body

System / part

Key point

Pleural cavity

space between the lungs and chest wall; filled with a thin film of pleural FLUID (a liquid)

Eye — three coats (outer→inner)

Sclera (fibrous) → Choroid (vascular) → Retina (nervous)

Vitamins

D from sunlight on skin; A (vision), C (scurvy), K (clotting), B-complex

Blood

groups A/B/AB/O & Rh; RBC (haemoglobin, oxygen), WBC (immunity), platelets (clotting)

Key organs

liver (detox/bile), kidney (filtration), pancreas (insulin), heart (4 chambers)

Botany

●        Roots: banyan's downward aerial support roots = PROP roots; tap vs fibrous vs adventitious roots

●        Photosynthesis & respiration: photosynthesis in chloroplasts (light → glucose + O₂); respiration releases energy (glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + ATP)

4 · Environment & Ecology

●        Permafrost thawing (Arctic): primary concern = release of METHANE and CO₂ (potent greenhouse gases) — amplifies warming

●        Ozone layer: in the stratosphere; depleted by CFCs; Montreal Protocol phases out ozone-depleting substances

●        Climate agreements: UNFCCC, Kyoto Protocol, Paris Agreement (1.5–2°C goal); India's net-zero target 2070

●        Pollution: particulate matter (PM2.5/PM10), AQI; eutrophication; bioaccumulation & biomagnification

Defence & technology

Item

Detail
